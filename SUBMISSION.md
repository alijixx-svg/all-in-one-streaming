# ALL IN ONE — Final Project Submission

## Project name

ALL IN ONE

## Idea summary

Problem: Viewers in Kuwait must explore streaming services separately and lack one simple local place to review a proposed multi-service offer. Users: Kuwait-based viewers interested in convenient streaming choices. Solution: ALL IN ONE is a responsive website for exploring a proposed bundle featuring Netflix, HBO Max, OSN, Shahid, and Hulu. Advantage: It presents a simple three-month demo offer in Kuwaiti dinars: KWD 2 total for new customers and KWD 4 total for returning customers. Project description: Visitors browse service pages and sample poster galleries, review the bundle, and try a simulated KNET checkout. The site collects no payment details and does not provide streaming playback or account access. Future plan: Confirm provider permissions, Kuwait availability, and authorized delivery before offering real subscriptions or payments; then add secure payments and renewal reminders.
